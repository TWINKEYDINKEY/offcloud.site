FAQ

Questions GPs ask before booking

Do patients need to agree to being recorded?

Yes. Just like any scribe, you ask the patient at the start of the consult. Recording only starts when you press record, and nothing ever leaves your practice.

Does it work with Best Practice or MedicalDirector?

Yes. You copy the approved note and paste it into Best Practice, MedicalDirector or any clinical software. Deeper Best Practice integration is on the way.

What does it cost?

You pay for offcloud, not for every consult. There are no per-consult or token fees. Pricing depends on the size of your practice, and we'll walk you through it on the demo call.

How many GPs can use it, and who sets it up?

Every GP in the practice can use it at the same time. We install and set up the box on site, so there's nothing for your team to configure.

What happens to the recordings?

They stay on the box in your practice and never touch the cloud. Audio can be deleted as soon as the note is approved, so only the note you signed off remains.
